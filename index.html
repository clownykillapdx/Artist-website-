The Story
CLOWNYKILLA & DJ FLIPPP — Studio Session · 808 Kartel Records

Who Is
CLOWNYKILLA?

CLOWNYKILLA began recording music at just twelve years old, long before any official releases. At sixteen, he made his debut with the single "Swervin" featuring a local artist, released on August 4th, 2022 across all major streaming platforms. While the early numbers were modest, the response pushed him to keep building rather than step away.

From there, he connected with local rappers and producers to form a creative collective known as OFF THE RIP (OTR) — a group made up of three rappers and two producers focused on developing a unified sound and vision.

During this era, Clownykilla and OTR member BIG OZ released the track "Klonopin" exclusively on SoundCloud. The record quickly gained underground traction, spreading through reposts, word of mouth, and organic community support.

Building on that momentum, he followed up with "Flash Yo Phone at My Eyes" and "Springfield Massachusetts USA," both released on all major platforms, further establishing his presence as an emerging artist with a distinct direction.

Shortly after, Clownykilla announced an upcoming EP under 808 Kartel Records, outlining his intent in the project description:

"Los Angeles has had numerous hitters when it comes to West Coast music. No one does it like LA — so I want to bring that here."

The Vision

Since then, Clownykilla has evolved beyond just releasing music — he's been building a lane. While not officially the "leader" of OFF THE RIP (OTR), he stands as a central creative force and the most visible face of a new West Coast-influenced wave emerging in the Portland metro scene.

His goal is to bridge two regions: Los Angeles and Portland. Where LA is known for its aggressive West Coast bounce and established sonic identity, Portland has yet to fully be recognized in that space. Clownykilla aims to change that.

Working alongside DJ FLIPPP and the 808 Kartel Records team, he's developing a sound rooted in West Coast tradition while pushing it into a new geographic lane. Several tracks are already completed and creatively locked in, currently awaiting final beat licensing clearance before release to all major DSPs. His approach is deliberate — not a rushed release cycle.

Why It Matters

Clownykilla represents a rare position for a Portland artist: direct connection to a platinum-level production network and an underground movement already gaining traction through SoundCloud, Instagram growth, and organic repost culture.

He isn't chasing trends. He's building momentum for a sound and scene that hasn't fully been claimed in his region. With the upcoming EP, Clownykilla is pushing a West Coast-driven identity into Portland with intention, structure, and presence. When the project drops, it's meant to be felt — not just heard.
