The Story
CLOWNYKILLA & DJ FLIPPP — Studio Session · 808 Kartel Records

Who Is
CLOWNYKILLA?

CLOWNYKILLA began recording music at just twelve years old, long before any official releases. At sixteen, he made his debut with the single "Swervin" featuring a local artist, released on August 4th, 2022 across all major streaming platforms.

From there, he connected with local rappers and producers to form a creative collective known as OFF THE RIP (OTR) — a group made up of three rappers and two producers focused on developing a unified sound and vision.

During this era, Clownykilla and OTR member BIG OZ released the track "Klonopin" exclusively on SoundCloud. The record quickly gained underground traction through reposts, word of mouth, and organic community support.

Building on that momentum, he followed up with "Flash Yo Phone at My Eyes" and "Springfield Massachusetts USA," both released on all major platforms.

"Los Angeles has had numerous hitters when it comes to West Coast music. No one does it like LA — so I want to bring that here."

The Vision

Working alongside DJ FLIPPP and the 808 Kartel Records team, he's developing a sound rooted in West Coast tradition while pushing it into a new geographic lane — Portland, Oregon.
